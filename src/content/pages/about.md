I'm Thang Pham (Phạm Nam Thắng). I have built web apps with Laravel and Vue.js for 10 years, since April 2016.

I lived and worked in New Zealand from 2021 to 2025, the last two years in Golden Bay. Now I'm based in Vietnam. I still work NZ and AU hours, and I'm open to moving back to NZ.

## How I work

You deal with me directly. There is no account manager in between.

You get a short written update at least once a week: what's done, what's next, and anything that is blocked.

I write tests before I change code that matters. On a large app, that is how you change things without breaking them.

I release in small steps that are easy to review and easy to roll back.

I use AI coding tools every day. They help me read large codebases and work faster. I review everything they write before it ships.
