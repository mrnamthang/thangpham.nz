## The problem

The product is a workforce management platform used by NZ businesses for rosters, timesheets, leave and billing. Over the years it grew to more than 500,000 lines of code on Laravel 9, Vue 2 and Inertia v1.

Vue 2 had reached end of life, and the old versions were blocking package updates and security fixes. The product could not stop for a rewrite.

## What I did

- Led the upgrade from Laravel 9 to 12, Vue 2 to 3 and Inertia v1 to v2.
- Broke the work into small steps, so each change could be tested and released on its own.
- Updated or replaced packages that did not support the new versions.
- Moved the front end to Vue 3 and fixed the breaking changes in Inertia v2.
- Reworked the UI and UX of profiles, rosters, timesheets, leave and billing.

## The result

The app now runs on Laravel 12, Vue 3 and Inertia v2. Customers kept using the product the whole time.
