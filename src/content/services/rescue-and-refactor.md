### What this looks like

- I read the code, talk to whoever knows it best, and write down how it works.
- I add tests around the parts that make money first: billing, payroll, core flows.
- I find the slowest pages and queries using real numbers, then fix them.
- I refactor in small steps. Each step is shipped and checked before the next one.

### A good fit if

- The developer who built the app has left.
- Small changes take weeks, or break something else.
- The app gets slower as your data grows.
