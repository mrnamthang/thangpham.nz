---
title: "Taking over and fixing an existing app"
order: 2
---

I take over codebases that have become hard to change. I read the code, write tests around the parts that make money, measure the slow pages on real data, and fix them in small releases.

On RosterIt, the roster review screen went from 44 seconds to about 2 seconds, and the test suite grew from 2 tests to about 3,500.

This is a good fit if the developer who built the app has left, small changes take weeks, or pages get slower as your data grows.
