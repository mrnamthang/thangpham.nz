### What this looks like

- Admin panels built with Laravel Filament, so your team can manage data, users and settings without a developer.
- REST APIs for mobile apps, partners or other systems.
- Integrations with payroll and accounting systems such as Xero, iPayroll and TechOne: pay code mapping, exports and imports.
- Golden master tests around exports, so a refactor never changes a payslip by accident.

### A good fit if

- Your team does manual work in spreadsheets that the app could do.
- You need to send data to a payroll or accounting system.
- You have an integration that breaks and nobody wants to touch it.
