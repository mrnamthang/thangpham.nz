I build admin panels in Laravel Filament, REST APIs, and exports to payroll and accounting systems.

I have built payroll exports for TechOne and iPayroll and rebuilt a Xero NZ Payroll sync, with tests that pin down the pay rules.

This is a good fit if your team does manual work in spreadsheets that the app could do, or you have an integration that breaks and nobody wants to touch it.
